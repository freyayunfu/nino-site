Joining a family

Families are invite-only. Ask someone already in the family to open their Family page and send you the 6-letter invite code; install Nino, sign up, and enter the code.

Installing for parents in mainland China

Nino is not yet listed on the China App Store. Have your family install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the TestFlight invite link we send you to install Nino. For Android phones, contact us for the installer.

What the host (Nino) is

An AI host that lives in your family's group: it asks one question a day, follows up, runs family trivia and hosts game night. It never nags and never counts streaks; when the family is quiet, it is quiet too. What it says is AI-generated and can be wrong — tell it so in your private thread, or turn off the daily question in the family settings.

Report, block, leave, delete

· Report: long-press a message → Report. We receive it and review within 24 hours.
· Block: Family page → long-press a member → Block. Their messages no longer show on your device.
· Leave a family: at the bottom of the Family page.
· Delete your account: Me → Delete account. Login, private thread and profile are erased immediately; see the privacy policy.

FAQ

No notifications? Settings → Notifications → Nino, make sure they are allowed. TestFlight builds notify the same way as the App Store build.
"Their day" card is empty? The other person has not set their place yet, or today's card has not been generated — it refreshes once each morning.
Game night will not connect? It needs network and microphone access; check Settings → Nino.
