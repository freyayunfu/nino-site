🧅 Nino Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

What Nino is

Nino is a private family chat app with an AI host that helps families talk.

What we collect

· Account: your email address, used to sign in.
· What you share: group messages, photos, voice and video messages, your private thread with Nino, your name, avatar, place and birthday.
· Place: the district or city you pick from a list. If you tap "Use my location", the phone takes one coarse location fix at that moment to pick it for you; precise coordinates never leave the phone. We store the place name and a coordinate rounded to about 10 km, used for weather.
· Activity (only if you turn on "Share activity"): your daily step count, exercise minutes and workouts (type and minutes) read from Apple Health, shared with the family you chose. Totals only: no heart rate, no routes, no sleep. Turning it off deletes what was shared; rows older than 90 days are deleted automatically.
· Usage data: anonymous product events (such as "opened the chat"), used only to improve the product.
We do not read your contacts or calendar, do not track your location continuously, show no ads, and never sell data.

AI processing and service providers

To let Nino host the conversation, some content is processed by the services below, each under its own terms and not used to train their public models:

Anthropic (Claude)reads group and private messages to host, ask questions and write memories; describes photos you send; sees shared daily activity totals as part of what the host reads
OpenAIdecides when Nino speaks during game night; looks up local weather and news by place name only (no chat content)
MiniMax / ElevenLabssynthesize Nino's voice; ElevenLabs also transcribes your speech during game night
LiveKitcarries game-night audio and video calls
Open-Meteoweather and place lookup by place name (no personal content)

Storage and security

Data is stored on Supabase in the United States, encrypted in transit, and isolated per family: only the members of your family can see your family's content.

Reporting and blocking

Long-press any message to report it; we receive every report and review it within 24 hours. Block a member from the Family page to hide their messages on your device. Families are invite-only: only someone with the invite code can join.

Deletion

Delete your account any time from inside the app: your login, private thread, device records and profile (place, birthday) are erased immediately. Messages you sent to the family group stay with the family, as when someone leaves a group chat. Contact us for further removal.
